Last night I had a bad dream in my bed. A little bird wanted to show me something, so it led a young lad across a red desert. The lad wore just one shoe and carried a white towel up a tall tower.

Nothing made sense. He held a small lamb beside a bright lamp, and he could not turn back — he still had ten long miles to go. The whole thing felt wrong, and it lasted very long.

Near my mouth I heard a mouse. I tried to wring the water from a ring on my finger. I got up, put on a plain dress, and said one word out loud: "world." That word is hard for me, and so is "word."

Then the bird spoke. "The desert is not your home," it said. "The dessert is." I laughed. I turned, took ten slow steps, and rang the little ring like a bell. Suddenly the whole world felt calm. It was a strange dream — a very vivid one — but a good dream, not a bad one.
