Edward is a young lad who wants to see the world. One day he saw an advert for a TV show and said just one word: "Wow." At ten o'clock he tied his red shoe, turned off the lamp, and led his little lamb down the road.

"Are these the fields where I belong?" he asked. "Ah," said the farmer, "they are, or at least they were." Edward's hair was dyed brown last week — not a perm, just colour. He earns a small salary washing a hot pan and chopping celery, and he wonders whether all that money should belong to him, or only ten coins of it.

Then he had to choose: live here on the quiet farm, or leave for the bright world. Now it was his turn to decide. He looked at them both, turned around, and let the red lamp light lead the way. Whatever he earns — ten coins or a full salary — the choice was all his.
